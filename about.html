Who we are

Built on experience. Committed to lasting change.

Hope Without Borders Foundation invests in education, healthcare, and the long-term strength of communities.

Our story

From humanitarian work
to sustained investment.

Experiences working with refugees across three countries, in medical and humanitarian settings, inspired the foundation.

Those experiences reinforced the importance of long-term solutions: places to learn, access to essential healthcare, and support that helps communities build greater independence.

We partner with organizations close to the communities they serve, starting with support for a community education center relocating from Turkey to Jarabulus, Syria.

Our mission

Education, health, and a future with opportunity.

To provide educational programs, foundational learning infrastructure, and essential healthcare support to orphans, refugees, and underserved communities globally, with a dedicated focus on empowering vulnerable youth through knowledge and restoring physical wellbeing.

Our intended geographic focus

Communities across borders.

Turkey, Jordan, Syria, Palestine, Sudan, and the United States are our intended areas of focus. This is not a list of active programs in every country; our first confirmed project is in Jarabulus, Syria.
