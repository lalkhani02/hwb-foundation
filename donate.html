Before you give

Can I donate online now?

Not yet. Online giving will open once our payment provider is connected.

Use the secure giving link above to view available donation options.

What will the first project support?

HWB is helping fund the opening of a community education center in Jarabulus. The partner organization operates the center, with planned Arabic and cultural reintegration, after-school support, and teacher development programs.

Will there be monthly giving?

We intend to offer monthly giving when the donation platform is ready. Available options will appear on the secure donation form.
