Our work / Healthcare

Wellbeing makes opportunity possible.

Essential healthcare support is part of our commitment to refugees and underserved communities.

Our mission

Health and education belong together.

Our founding experiences in medical and humanitarian settings showed how closely health, learning, and opportunity are connected. Our mission includes helping restore physical wellbeing alongside educational support.

Healthcare is a future area of work.

We have not yet announced a healthcare project. Our first confirmed project is the Community Education Center preparing to open in Jarabulus, Syria.
