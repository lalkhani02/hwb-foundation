Privacy

Your privacy matters.

This page describes the current informational website.

Browsing this website

Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes. Our donation page loads CharityStack’s integration script to support online giving. Your browser connects to CharityStack when the script loads, which may disclose your IP address and browser information to that provider.

Website fonts

Our typography is loaded through Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to retrieve these font files, which may disclose your IP address and browser information to Google.

Online donations

Our donation form is provided by CharityStack, with payment processing through Stripe. Information you submit in the form is sent to these providers to process your gift. HWB can access donation records and donor details in CharityStack to administer donations and communicate with supporters. Payment details are entered through the payment provider, not stored in this website’s source files. Review the privacy information displayed in the donation checkout before submitting your gift.
