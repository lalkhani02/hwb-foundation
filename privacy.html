Browsing this website

This version of our site has no contact forms, donor accounts, or analytics scripts added by HWB. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes.

Website fonts

Our typography is loaded through Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to retrieve these font files, which may disclose your IP address and browser information to Google.
